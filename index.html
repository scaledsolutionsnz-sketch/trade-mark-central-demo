About Trade Mark Central

A straight-talking local builder you can actually get hold of.

Trade Mark Central is a Central Otago building trade run out of Alexandra. The idea is simple: deal directly with the person doing the work, get an honest price, and have the job done properly and tidily.

From new builds and renovations through to decks, fences and the maintenance jobs that keep piling up, you get one point of contact from the first quote to the final walk-through — no call centres, no runaround.
