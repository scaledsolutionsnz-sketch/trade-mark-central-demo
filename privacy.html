Privacy Policy

This is placeholder content for the demo website. A final privacy policy will be supplied by Trade Mark Central before the site goes live.

1. Overview

Trade Mark Central respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle any personal information you share with us when enquiring about building, renovation or property maintenance work.

2. What we collect

When you contact us by phone or text, we may collect your name, contact details and information about the work you're enquiring about. We only collect what we need to quote and carry out the job.

3. How we use it

Your information is used to respond to your enquiry, prepare quotes, carry out work and keep in touch about your project. We do not sell your information to anyone.

4. Sharing

We may share relevant details with trusted trade partners (such as electricians or plumbers) only where needed to complete your job, and with suppliers to order materials.

5. Storage & access

We take reasonable steps to keep your information secure. Under the Privacy Act 2020 you can ask to see the personal information we hold about you and request corrections.

6. Contact

Any privacy questions? Call or text Mark on 021 114 7447.
